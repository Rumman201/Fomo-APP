# Incident Management & Operational Runbooks Skill

## Purpose
Establishes standardized procedures, escalation pathways, and operational runbooks for production incidents.

## Core Directives
1. Maintain actionable runbooks in `docs/operations/runbooks/` for app outages, API degradation, DB corruption, and key compromise.
2. Define exact procedures for Google Play Console rollout halting and forward-rolling hotfixes.
3. Require post-mortem documentation within 24 hours of P0/P1 incidents.
